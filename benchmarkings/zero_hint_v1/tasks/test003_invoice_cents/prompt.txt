The repository computes invoice money as integer cents.

Behaviour required by the public tests:

- `line_cents(quantity, unit_price)` returns the charge for one line.
- `invoice_cents(lines)` returns the sum of every line.
- `quantity` is a non-negative integer.
- `unit_price` is a decimal string with a dollars part and exactly two cents digits (example shape: `"19.99"`).
- Results are integer cents. The sum of line results must equal the invoice result.
- Invalid quantity or price shape raises `ValueError`.

Inspect the workspace, change only `invoicing.py`, and make the public tests pass. Use the read, search, patch, and test tools. Patch arguments are a workspace-relative `path` and a unified diff in `diff`. Test arguments are an `argv` array, for example `["python3", "-m", "unittest", "discover", "-s", "tests"]`.
