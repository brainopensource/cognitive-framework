# ADR-0015: Promotion is a partial order over a frontier, not a scalar objective

**Reasoning.** A scalar is self-reinforcing through the corpus: what it rewards becomes what is recorded, trained on, and optimised harder

**Reversal condition.** A domain where every dimension is genuinely commensurable — none is known
